# 🧠 Object-Oriented Programming (OOP-F22) — C++ Labs

A complete index of all **C++ lab tasks** and **object-oriented programming assignments**, ranging from basic class design to inheritance, polymorphism, templates, and abstract classes.

---

## 📘 LAB 2 — Classes & Object Basics
1. **Date Class** – Initialize, validate, and display day/month/year; calculate user age.  
2. **Employee Class** – Employee code, age at joining/current age; calculate tenure ≥ 2 years.  
3. **Triangle Class** – Base, height, area calculation; find triangle with greatest area.  
4. **ComplexNumber Class** – Real + imaginary parts; display in form `a + bi`.

---

## 🐱 LAB 3 — Constructors, Destructors, Copying
1. **Cat Class** – Param + copy constructors, destructor, getters/setters, display with memory-safe handling.  
2. **Student Class** – Includes `Date` object; constructors, destructor, display full info.  
3. **Employee Class (Revisited)** – Includes `Date`; constructors, destructor, tenure calc.

---

## 🧾 LAB 5 — Static Members & Deep Copy
1. **SavingsAccount** – Shared interest rate, calculate monthly interest, modify global rate.  
2. **Time Class** – 24-hour format; default + param + copy constructors; tick(), showTime().  
3. **Item Store** – Manage items, expiration, price drop if <20 days remain.  
4. **NextBridge Software House** – Calculate employee increment based on experience.  
5. **Custom String Library** – Rebuild 7+ functions from `<string.h>` manually.

---

## 🚗 LAB 6 — Constructors, Rule of Three
1. **Car Class** – Name, model, doors; constructors, destructors, getters/setters, display.  
2. **Employee Class (Hourly)** – Pay rate + working hours; AddSal() & AddWork() logic.  
3. **Complex Class (Operator Overloading)** – Implement `+`, `-`, `*`, `/` operators.

---

## ⏱️ LAB 7 — Operator Overloading (Time & Date)
1. **Time Class** – Implement Rule of Three + `==`, `>`, `<`, `<<`, `>>`, `+`, `-`, `++`, `--`.  
2. **Date Class** – Similar to Time; add/subtract days, increment/decrement operators.

---

## 🚕 LAB 8 — Composition: Uber Simulation
1. **Driver, Rider, Ride Classes** – Contain all trip info, fare calculation, promo code discounts, and display combined trip + participant data.

---

## 📦 LAB 10 (V1) — Composition Practice
1. **Product & ReleaseDate Classes** – Store product name, price, and release date.  
2. **People, DOB, MyString, Address Classes** – Nested composition structure.  
3. **MyChar & MyString Classes** – Operator overloading (`<<`, `>>`, `+`, `++`, `--`) with deep copy.

## 🏤 LAB 10 (V2) — Inheritance Hierarchy
1. **Package_PPO (Base)** → **NormalPackage**, **TwoDayPackage**, **OvernightPackage** – Cost calculation via inheritance.  
2. **Multiple Inheritance: Person + Employee → Teacher** – Combine attributes from both parents.

---

## 🧩 LAB 11 — Access Control & Advanced Inheritance
1. **Shapes Hierarchy** – Pointers, constructors via initializer list, display & area.  
2. **Cards Hierarchy** – Base `Card`; derived `CallingCard`, `IDCard`, `DrivingLicense`.  
3. **BankAccount Hierarchy** – Savings vs Checking with overridden deposit/withdraw.  
4. **Multiple Inheritance: Teacher (Person + Employee)** – Extended pay scale demo.

## 🌀 LAB 12 & LAB 14 — Abstract Classes & Polymorphism
1. **Shape (Abstract Base)** → **Point → Circle → Cylinder** – Virtual functions for area & volume; polymorphic calls.  
2. **Pizza & PizzaOrder** – Class composition + cost calculation.  
3. **Function Templates** – Generic sort functions (ascending/descending).

## 🔮 LAB 13 (GRADED 5) — Polymorphism & Templates
1. **Shape Hierarchy** – Abstract base with polymorphic area methods.  
2. **Function Template (`myMax`)** – Generic comparison for int, double, string.  
3. **Creature Hierarchy** – `Creature` → `Player`, `Monster` → `WildPig`, `Dragon`; abstract methods & polymorphism demo.
